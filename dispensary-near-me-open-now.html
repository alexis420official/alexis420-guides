Dispensary Near Me Open Now? Do This Before You Drive

Bottom line: an "open now" result is only as good as the hours behind it. Check that the store is state licensed, check how long until it closes, and call if it's under half an hour.

Here's a quick routine that keeps you from pulling up to a dark storefront.

1. Search licensed stores only

General map apps mix dispensaries with smoke shops and CBD stores. A licensed dispensary is on its state's official license list, full stop. Alexis 420 lists only those, and its open-now finder checks every nearby store's posted hours against the time where you are:

https://alexis420.com/guides/dispensary-near-me-open-now/

Tap "Find what's open", allow location, and you get the closest spots open right now, each with the time it closes today.

2. Read the closing time

Across licensed stores, the typical Friday close is around 9:45 PM, and most weekday hours run about 9 AM to 9 PM. If the finder says a place closes at 9 and it's 8:40, call. Most stores stop sales a few minutes early.

3. Know your state's hard stop

Some states cut sales off by law no matter what the store wants:

  • Hawaii and Kentucky: 8 PM
  • Florida: 9 PM
  • California, Oregon, Maine, Connecticut, Delaware, Arkansas: 10 PM
  • Maryland, Ohio and DC: 11 PM
  • Colorado and Washington: midnight

If you're in one of those and it's past the cutoff, nothing is open, and anyone selling is not licensed.

4. Bring the right stuff

A valid 21+ photo ID (plus a patient card in medical-only states), and cash or debit.

Sundays

96% of licensed dispensaries with posted hours are open on Sunday, typically 9 AM to about 8:50 PM. Connecticut and Delaware run shorter Sunday hours by law.
