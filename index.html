Dispensary vs. Smoke Shop: What's the Difference?

Quick answer: a licensed dispensary holds a state cannabis license and sells lab-tested cannabis. A smoke shop sells pipes, papers, vapes and hemp products, and cannot legally sell state-regulated cannabis.

Licensed dispensary

  • On the state's license list.
  • Checks ID at the door, 21+ (or registered patients).
  • Products carry state labels, batch numbers and lab results.

Smoke shop

  • Sells accessories and hemp-derived products.
  • Rules for hemp products vary widely by state.
  • Some shops sell cannabis without a license. Those products are untested.

How to tell

Look the shop up on the state's license list, ask for the license number, and check product labels for a batch number and lab result.
